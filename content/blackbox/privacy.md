Black Box is a game in which you fill a box with ink as fast as you can. This policy explains what data the
game uses, why, and the choices you have.

## What the game stores on your device

- Your settings (music, sounds, vibration, ad-privacy choice), your best times, your progress (which Ranked
  boards you have unlocked, Practice tries) and Ranked results waiting to be uploaded.
- This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## What is sent to our servers (Unity Gaming Services)

When you are online, the game uses Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies, to run the
leaderboards:

- **An anonymous player ID**, created automatically. You do not need an account, email or password.
- **Your player name.** It is generated at random, and you can change it in the YOU tab. It is shown on the
  public leaderboards.
- **Your Ranked results:** time, difficulty, date, and basic stroke statistics used to detect cheating.
  Times appear on the public daily, weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboards.

We use this data only to run the leaderboards and to keep them fair. We do not sell it.

## Advertising

The game is free and shows only **rewarded videos that you choose to watch** (for an extra Ranked try or more
Practice tries). Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks, including Unity Ads. To show and
measure ads, these services may collect:

- your device's advertising ID;
- device and app information (model, OS version, language, approximate location from the IP address);
- ad interactions (views, clicks).

**Your choice:** the first time you play, the game asks whether ad partners may use this data for personalized
ads. You can change this at any time in **YOU → Ad privacy**. If you choose "non-personalized only", ads are
still shown but are not personalized. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's
settings.

The ad partners process this data under their own privacy policies:

- Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- LevelPlay (Unity): https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/

## Children

Black Box is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the data linked to your player ID by writing to office@fofensoft.com. If you are
in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your data
protection authority.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address, with a new effective date.
