# Black Box — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026
**Developer:** Fofensoft
**Contact:** office@fofensoft.com

Black Box is a game in which you fill a box with ink as fast as you can. This policy explains what data the
game uses, why, and the choices you have.

## What the game stores on your device

- Your settings (music, sounds, vibration, mods, ad-privacy and stats choices), your best times, your progress
  (which levels and Ranked boards you have unlocked, how many Practice runs you played today) and Ranked results
  waiting to be uploaded.
- This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## What is sent to our servers (Unity Gaming Services)

When you are online, the game uses Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies, to run the
leaderboards:

- **An anonymous player ID**, created automatically. You do not need an account, email or password.
- **Your player name.** It is generated at random, and you can change it in the YOU tab. It is shown on the
  public leaderboards.
- **Your Ranked results:** time, difficulty, date, the mods you played with, and basic stroke statistics used to
  detect cheating. Times (and the mods used) appear on the public daily, weekly, monthly and all-time
  leaderboards.

We use this data only to run the leaderboards and to keep them fair. We do not sell it.

## Anonymous gameplay statistics (optional)

When **"Share anonymous stats"** is on, the game sends usage statistics to Unity Analytics, part of Unity
Gaming Services:

- how you play: runs started and finished, times, difficulty, mods, where runs end early, levels unlocked,
  ad breaks and video offers, leaderboard views, shares and renames;
- technical data collected by Unity Analytics: an anonymous player ID, device model, OS version, language,
  country (from the IP address) and session times.

We use it only to see which parts of the game work and to balance the levels. We do not sell it and do not use
it for advertising.

- **In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland** it is off until you say yes: the privacy
  screen at first launch asks you ("Help us balance the game"), with "Yes, happy to help" and "No, thanks".
- **Everywhere else** it is on by default. The privacy screen at first launch shows the switch, and you can turn
  it off there or any time later.

You can change it any time in **YOU → Anonymous stats**. If you switch it off, collection stops and the game asks
Unity to delete the statistics already sent. The game guesses your region from your phone's SIM, network,
region setting and time zone; if any of them points to Europe, it asks first.
Unity's policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

## Advertising

The game is free. It shows **short ad breaks between Practice runs** (the first runs of each day are free) and
**rewarded videos that you choose to watch** (for an extra Ranked try or for Practice runs without ads).
Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks, including Unity Ads and Google AdMob. To show and
measure ads, these services may collect:

- your device's advertising ID;
- device and app information (model, OS version, language, approximate location from the IP address);
- ad interactions (views, clicks).

**Your choice:** the first time you play, the game asks whether ad partners may use this data for personalized
ads. You can change this at any time in **YOU → Ad privacy**. If you choose "non-personalized only", ads are
still shown but are not personalized. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's
settings.

The ad partners process this data under their own privacy policies:

- Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- LevelPlay (Unity): https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/
- Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

Black Box is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the data linked to your player ID by writing to office@fofensoft.com. If you are
in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your data
protection authority.
