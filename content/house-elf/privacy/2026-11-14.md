## 11. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES
Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.

## 13. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?
In Short: House Elf is designed for families, so a household may include children under 13. We collect children's information only with verifiable parental consent, using methods recognized under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and its implementing rule (16 C.F.R. Part 312), matched to how sensitive the information is.

A child cannot access the Services on their own. A child can only join a household when an adult member of that household who has completed parental verification (described below) generates a one-time invite code within their own account and gives it to the child.

**Two layers of parental consent**

1. **Household participation (all households, free or Premium).** Before an adult can invite their first child, that adult must complete email-plus verification: we send a verification email to the adult's account email address, and the adult confirms, by following the link, that they are at least 18 years old and are the parent or legal guardian of the children they will invite. After confirmation we send a second email confirming that consent was recorded and explaining how to withdraw it. Each adult in a household verifies separately.
2. **Photograph-based avatar (Premium households only).** Creating a child's avatar from a photograph of the child is available only in households with an active paid House Elf Premium subscription, purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play by the adult who created the household (the "founder"). Only the founder can grant photograph consent, separately for each child, immediately before any photograph is uploaded, and only after device-level authentication (fingerprint, face recognition, or device passcode) on the founder's own device. The paid subscription transaction, processed by Apple or Google and associated with the founder's account, together with this device-level authentication, is the method we rely on for this layer. No photograph is transmitted, processed, or stored unless photograph consent has been granted for that specific child. In households without Premium, and whenever photograph consent is declined or withdrawn, the child receives an AI-generated avatar created from a generic reference image instead; no photograph of the child is used.

**Information we collect about children under 13**

With household participation consent, we collect:
- a display name, chosen by the child on their own device or entered by the adult (it need not be the child's real name);
- an age category (for example child or teen) selected by the adult;
- activity data about household chores, duties, rewards, and reputation within the household, used solely to operate the household features visible to the family;
- persistent identifiers (such as an app installation identifier and a push notification token) used solely to support the internal operations of the Services (session management, security, notifications, crash diagnostics, and core functionality).

Only after photograph consent has been granted (Premium households), we additionally process:
- one photograph, uploaded to generate the child's AI character avatar. The photograph is sent to our AI service provider for processing, is never stored on our own infrastructure, and is deleted from the provider's systems within approximately one hour;
- the resulting AI-generated avatar, a stylized, non-photorealistic illustration, which is kept for as long as the child remains a member of the household.

We do not collect from children: precise geolocation, contact information, biometric data, audio or video recordings, or browsing history outside the Services. Children's sessions are excluded from our product analytics (Amplitude) and from our mobile measurement provider (AppsFlyer, which is never initialized on children's devices), and no advertising identifiers (IDFA or Google Advertising ID) are collected from or associated with children's sessions.

**How we use children's information**

Solely to provide the core functionality of the Services (chores, rewards, reputation, avatar display), to generate and display the child's avatar within the household, to notify household members about household activity, and to keep the Services secure. We do not use children's information for behavioral advertising, marketing, profiling, or cross-context tracking, and we do not sell or share children's information for advertising purposes.

**Disclosure of children's information to service providers**

We disclose limited information about children only to service providers acting on our behalf, under written agreements, and only for providing the Services:
- AI avatar generation: Replicate, Inc. (United States), which hosts the image model (developed by Black Forest Labs) that turns the photograph into an avatar — only where photograph consent has been granted. The photograph is deleted from Replicate's systems within approximately one hour and is not used to train models. The generated avatar image (not the photograph) is processed by Recraft AI to remove its background.
- Backend infrastructure: Supabase, Inc. stores account information, household activity, generated avatars, and consent records.
- Notifications: Apple Push Notification Service and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver notifications to household devices, including a child's device; notification content is limited to household activity.
- Crash diagnostics: Sentry processes technical error reports from household devices, including a child's device, with user identifiers removed.
- Payments: Apple Inc. and Google LLC process Premium subscription payments on behalf of the founder; no children's information is disclosed to them.

**Consent records**

Each consent decision is recorded in an audit trail: for household participation, the adult's verified email address, the attestation they confirmed, and the date and time; for photograph consent, the disclosure text and version shown, the verification signals collected (we store only whether device authentication succeeded — never biometric data), and the date and time. These records are kept for the life of the account and for any longer period required by law.

**Parental rights**

A parent or legal guardian in the household may, at any time:
- review the personal information we hold about their child;
- correct inaccurate information;
- request deletion of the child's information;
- withdraw consent and refuse further collection or use.

Any parent in the household can withdraw a child's photograph consent in the app (Settings > Photo permissions); doing so deletes the photo-based avatar. An adult can withdraw their household participation consent in the app (Settings > Child permissions); this prevents that adult from inviting further children, while children already in the household remain unless their information is deleted. Parents can also contact us at privacy@fofensoft.com. After a withdrawal or deletion request, we delete the child's personal information, including AI-generated avatars, from our active systems within thirty (30) days and instruct our service providers to do the same, except for records we must keep by law (such as the record of the consent decision itself).

**Children outside the United States**

For children in the European Economic Area, we comply with Article 8 of the GDPR and applicable national rules: where a household member is below the digital consent age in their country (between 13 and 16, depending on the Member State), we process their personal data only on the basis of consent from a parent or legal guardian, given as described above. For children in Canada, we comply with PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy law.

**Questions about children's privacy**

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without a parent's knowledge or consent, contact us at privacy@fofensoft.com. We will investigate promptly and delete the information if we cannot verify parental consent.
